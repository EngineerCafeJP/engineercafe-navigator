# Engineer Cafe Reception Narration (English / 5 slides)

Narration script for the English reception deck (`engineer-cafe-en.pdf`). Designed to be read aloud to visitors arriving at the Engineer Cafe reception. Each slide takes roughly 30–45 seconds at a natural speaking pace.

---

## Slide 1: Welcome to Engineer Cafe

Welcome to Engineer Cafe.
The Engineer Cafe was established in August 2019 as part of the "Engineer Friendly City Fukuoka" initiative — a collaboration between Fukuoka City and its residents to make our city a great place for engineers to live and work.

This facility supports engineers, people involved in engineering, and anyone aspiring to become an engineer. Our community managers are on site every day, ready to help with any small questions or concerns you might have.

For more specialized topics, we are also supported by many "Hacker Supporters" — active engineers who volunteer their expertise. So please don't hesitate to reach out whenever you run into something technical you'd like to discuss.

---

## Slide 2: Co-working Spaces

Our main hall and underground areas are primarily run as an "Event Space for Engineers." However, when no events are scheduled, we open these areas as free co-working spaces for our visitors.

The co-working spaces available to you are: the Main Hall, the Under Space, and the Concentration Space.

The Concentration Space, shown on the right, is a quiet area divided into six small rooms. As it is a designated quiet area, please refrain from talking, taking phone calls, or holding online meetings inside.

If you would like to change seats during your visit, please always let the reception desk know first.

We also have a Meeting Space for small group discussions, and a Maker's Space designed for hands-on engineering projects. Please feel free to ask our staff for more details.

---

## Slide 3: Usage Time and Extensions

Seats are generally available in two-hour time slots. The soundproof room is the only exception — those slots are one hour long.

If no one is waiting, you are welcome to extend your time as many times as you like. Please let us know as early as possible when you would like to extend.

To request an extension, you can either speak to a staff member directly, or post in the reception channel of our Online Engineer Cafe on Discord.

The Online Engineer Cafe is a community space where engineers and engineering enthusiasts can connect with one another. We highly encourage you to join. Our staff will explain how to join Discord later, so please feel free to ask.

Note that the soundproof room is a small, single-person space.

---

## Slide 4: Food and Drinks

Please note that the Fukuoka City Red Brick Cultural Center, which houses our facility, is registered as a nationally Important Cultural Property. Because of this, eating and drinking are generally not permitted inside the building.

There is one exception: beverages with a sealed lid may be brought in. However, even with a lid, drinks consumed through a straw are not allowed, so please keep this in mind.

If you have any questions about what you can or cannot bring in, please ask our staff.
